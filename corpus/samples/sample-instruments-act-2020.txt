THE SAMPLE INSTRUMENTS ACT, 2020 (ILLUSTRATIVE)

An illustrative Act of the fictional territory of Sampleland, written so that the section-aware
segmenter has chapters, sections, sub-sections and clauses to find. Nothing in it states or
paraphrases the law of any real jurisdiction.

CHAPTER I  PRELIMINARY

1. Short title and commencement.

(1) This illustrative Act may be called the Sample Instruments Act, 2020.

(2) It extends to the whole of the fictional territory of Sampleland and comes into force on such
date as the illustrative authority may appoint.

2. Definitions.

In this illustrative Act, unless the context otherwise requires, the following expressions carry the
meanings given to them here.

(a) "illustrative authority" means the body named in Chapter II of this fixture;

(b) "sample article" means a thing to which this fixture applies;

(c) "register" means the record of sample articles maintained under this fixture.

CHAPTER II  THE ILLUSTRATIVE AUTHORITY

3. Constitution of the illustrative authority.

(1) There shall be an illustrative authority for the purposes of this fixture, constituted in such
manner as may be prescribed by the fixture rules.

(2) The illustrative authority shall consist of a chairperson and such other members as may be
appointed, and shall meet at such intervals as it considers necessary for the discharge of its
functions under this fixture.

(3) No act or proceeding of the illustrative authority shall be called in question merely on the
ground of a vacancy in, or a defect in the constitution of, the authority.

4. Functions of the illustrative authority.

(1) The illustrative authority shall maintain the register, determine applications made to it, and
publish its determinations in such manner as may be prescribed.

(2) In discharging its functions the illustrative authority shall have regard to the following
matters, and to nothing else that is not mentioned in this fixture.

(a) the completeness of the particulars furnished with an application;

(b) whether the sample article to which the application relates falls within a class to which this
fixture applies;

(c) whether the applicant has furnished such fee as may be prescribed;

(d) whether any earlier entry in the register relates to the same sample article;

(e) any representation made to the illustrative authority by a person appearing to it to have an
interest in the application.

(3) Where the illustrative authority refuses an application it shall record its reasons in writing
and communicate them to the applicant within such period as may be prescribed, and the applicant may
apply to the illustrative authority for a review of that refusal within thirty days of the
communication.

CHAPTER III  REGISTRATION

5. Application for registration.

(1) A person who wishes to have a sample article entered in the register shall apply to the
illustrative authority in such form and on payment of such fee as may be prescribed by the fixture
rules made under this illustrative Act.

(2) An application under sub-section (1) shall be accompanied by such particulars of the sample
article as are sufficient to identify it, and by a statement of the class within which the applicant
considers the sample article to fall.

(3) Where an application does not contain the particulars required by sub-section (2), the
illustrative authority may require the applicant to furnish them within such period as it may
specify, and may refuse the application if they are not furnished within that period. In determining
whether particulars are sufficient the illustrative authority shall have regard to the purpose for
which the register is maintained, namely to enable a person consulting it to identify a sample
article without recourse to any other record, and shall not require particulars beyond what that
purpose demands. Nothing in this sub-section entitles the illustrative authority to require a
particular that is not prescribed, and a requirement made otherwise than in accordance with this
sub-section is of no effect for the purposes of this fixture. Where a requirement is made under this
sub-section the illustrative authority shall record the requirement and the reason for it, and shall
furnish a copy of that record to the applicant on request and without charge.

6. Effect of registration.

(1) An entry in the register is evidence of the particulars entered in it and of nothing else.

(2) Registration under this fixture does not confer any right in respect of a sample article beyond
those conferred by this fixture, and does not affect any right arising otherwise than under it.

CHAPTER IV  LABELLING OF SAMPLE ARTICLES

7. Particulars to be borne on a label.

(1) Every sample article offered for supply shall bear a label containing the particulars specified
in sub-section (2).

(2) The particulars are the name of the sample article, the name and address of the person by whom
it was made, the entry number in the register where it is registered, and the date on which it was
made.

(3) A label shall not contain any claim as to the effect of a sample article that is not supported by
particulars furnished to the illustrative authority under section 5.
