SAMPLE CONVENTION ON ILLUSTRATIVE MATTERS, 2019 (ILLUSTRATIVE)

An illustrative convention between fictional parties, written so that the article-aware segmenter
has articles and paragraphs to find. It states no obligation of any real state.

PART I  GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1  Scope

1. This illustrative Convention applies as between the fictional parties to it, and to nothing else.

2. Nothing in this illustrative Convention affects any instrument in force between any real parties.

Article 2  Use of terms

1. For the purposes of this illustrative Convention, "party" means a fictional party that has
expressed its consent to be bound by it.

2. The expressions used in this illustrative Convention carry the meanings given to them in this
Article and carry no meaning drawn from any other instrument.

PART II  SUBSTANTIVE PROVISIONS

Article 3  Undertaking of the parties

1. Each party undertakes to give effect to this illustrative Convention in accordance with its own
fictional procedures, and to inform the other parties of the measures it has taken.

2. A party may make a declaration in respect of any provision of this illustrative Convention at the
time of expressing its consent to be bound, and may withdraw that declaration at any time by
notification to the depositary. A declaration made under this paragraph has effect only in respect of
the party making it, and does not affect the obligations of any other party under this illustrative
Convention. Where a declaration is withdrawn, the withdrawal takes effect on the first day of the
month following the expiry of three months after the date of receipt of the notification by the
depositary, and the depositary shall inform the other parties accordingly.

3. Nothing in this Article requires a party to take a measure that is inconsistent with its own
fictional constitutional arrangements.

Article 4  Settlement of differences

1. A difference between parties concerning the interpretation of this illustrative Convention shall
be settled by consultation between them.

2. Where consultation does not settle the difference within six months, the parties may by agreement
refer it to such fictional procedure as they may choose.
