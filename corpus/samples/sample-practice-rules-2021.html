SAMPLE FIXTURE PRACTICE RULES, 2021 (ILLUSTRATIVE)

SAMPLE FIXTURE DOCUMENT. Not law. Not a real instrument. Written to exercise the ingestion pipeline, and in particular the HTML parser.

PART I PRELIMINARY

1. Short title.

These illustrative rules may be called the Sample Fixture Practice Rules, 2021, and are made for the purposes of the fictional Sample Instruments Act, 2020.

2. Application.

(1) These illustrative rules apply to an application made to the illustrative authority under section 5 of the fictional Act.

(2) They do not apply to anything else, and confer no right on any person.

PART II PROCEDURE

3. Form of application.

(1) An application shall be made in the form set out in the schedule to these illustrative rules and shall be accompanied by the fee specified in that schedule.

(2) An application may be delivered to the illustrative authority by hand, by post, or by such electronic means as the illustrative authority may from time to time specify by notice published on its fictional website.

(3) Where an application is delivered by electronic means it is treated as received on the day on which it is transmitted, unless it is transmitted after the close of business, in which case it is treated as received on the next working day.

4. Manufacture and premises.

(1) A person who manufactures a sample article for supply shall hold a licence granted under these illustrative rules, and shall manufacture only at premises specified in that licence.

(2) The illustrative authority may inspect premises specified in a licence at any reasonable time, and may require the licensee to furnish such records relating to manufacture as may be prescribed.

(3) A licence granted under this rule is valid for such period as may be specified in it, and may be renewed on application made before it expires. On an application for renewal the illustrative authority shall have regard to whether the licensee has complied with the conditions of the licence during the period for which it was granted, whether the premises remain suitable for the manufacture of the sample articles to which the licence relates, and whether the records required to be kept under sub-rule (2) have been kept. Where the illustrative authority refuses a renewal it shall record its reasons in writing and communicate them to the licensee, and the licensee may apply for a review of that refusal within thirty days of the communication.

5. Fees.

The fee for an application, and the fee for the renewal of a licence, are those specified in the schedule to these illustrative rules.
