## India: patents and other IP
- The Patents Act, 1970 (as amended): s.2(1)(j) invention; s.2(1)(ja) inventive step; s.3(d), 3(e), 3(p); s.10(4)(d)(ii)(D) source and geographical origin of biological material; s.25(1)(j) and (k) opposition grounds; s.39 foreign filing by residents.
- The Patents Rules, 2003 as amended, including the Patents (Amendment) Rules, 2024. Official forms that mention biological material: identify from the forms themselves, never assume.
- IP India guidelines on patent applications relating to traditional knowledge and biological material: confirm exact title, year and current status.
- Trade Marks Act 1999 and Rules; Designs Act 2000 and Rules; Geographical Indications Act 1999 and Rules; Copyright Act 1957: core definition and registration provisions only, for the IP Protection Map.

## India: AYUSH drug regulation
- Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940: s.3 definitions of Ayurvedic, Siddha or Unani drug and of patent or proprietary medicine.
- Drugs Rules, 1945: Rule 158B; Schedule T; ASU labelling rules; Rule 170 (confirm current status, see FLAGSHIP_CASE.md #6); First Schedule list of authoritative books.
- Ministry of Ayush official notices and guidance; e-AUSHADHI portal information; Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia and Formulary of India (metadata and publicly usable parts only).

## India: food
- Food Safety and Standards (Ayurveda Aahara) Regulations, 2022, plus later official amendments, orders and FSSAI clarifications.

## India: biodiversity and ABS
- Biological Diversity Act, 2002: s.2 definitions, s.3, s.6, s.7, both as originally enacted and as amended.
- Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023; Biological Diversity Rules, 2024.
- The currently notified NBA access-and-benefit-sharing regulations (the 2014 regulations and any replacement: confirm which is in force); NBA forms and e-filing pages; NBA notices on IP applications.

## Traditional knowledge

- TKDL access model: patent-office access agreements and any wider or paid access approved after 2022. Describe it exactly as the official page says. The app itself never has TKDL database access.

## International
- Paris Convention (Article 4 priority); PCT treaty text and WIPO PCT basics (international application, national phase); Madrid (trade marks) and Hague (designs) overview pages.
- WIPO Treaty on Intellectual Property, Genetic Resources and Associated Traditional Knowledge (adopted 2024): text and status page with adopted date, entry-into-force status and contracting parties.
- Convention on Biological Diversity and the Nagoya Protocol: texts and India's party status from cbd.int.
